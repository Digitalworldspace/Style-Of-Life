Our Story

Style Of Life started as a small studio with one idea: jewelry and festive wear should feel personal, not mass-produced. Alongside our everyday fine jewelry, we handpick heavy sarees for weddings and celebrations.

Jewelry orders are placed through Etsy, backed by Etsy's buyer protection and secure checkout. Saree orders are confirmed directly over WhatsApp so we can get your fabric, colour and blouse size right — and we personally pack, check, and ship every order ourselves.
