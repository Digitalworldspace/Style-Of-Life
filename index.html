Our Story

Style Of Life started in Surat, India — a city known for generations of fine goldwork. We took that craft and built it around a simple idea: certified 14K gold jewelry that's personal enough to wear every day, starting with an initial that's yours alone. Each pendant, ring and chain is finished by hand before it ships to a customer somewhere else in the world.

Today our letter pendants travel from our workshop to homes across the globe, and we're building out a wider collection — rings, bracelets, hoops and made-to-order pieces — for anyone who wants their jewelry to feel like it was made with them in mind.
